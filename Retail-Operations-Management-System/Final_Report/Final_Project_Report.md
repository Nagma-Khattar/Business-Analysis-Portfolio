# 1. Executive Summary

The Retail Operations Management System (ROMS) is a Business Analysis portfolio project developed for NorthStar Retail Inc. The project demonstrates the complete business analysis lifecycle, from requirements gathering and documentation to process modeling, agile planning, testing, and user interface design.

The primary objective of the project is to improve retail operations by providing an integrated system for user management, inventory management, sales processing, customer management, and business reporting.

---

# 2. Business Problem

NorthStar Retail Inc. relied on manual processes for inventory tracking, sales recording, and customer management. These processes resulted in:

- Inventory inaccuracies
- Delayed reporting
- Inefficient sales tracking
- Limited visibility into business performance
- Increased operational effort

---

# 3. Proposed Solution

Develop a Retail Operations Management System that provides:

- Secure user authentication
- Inventory management
- Sales transaction processing
- Customer profile management
- Sales and business reporting

The solution improves operational efficiency while providing accurate and timely business information.
