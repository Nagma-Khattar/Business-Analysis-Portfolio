# Purpose

The Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) ensures that every business requirement is traced through functional requirements, user stories, use cases, implementation, and testing. It helps verify that all requirements are addressed and validated.

# Benefits

- Ensures complete requirements coverage.
- Supports impact analysis during change requests.
- Assists testers in verifying requirement implementation.
- Improves project quality and compliance.
